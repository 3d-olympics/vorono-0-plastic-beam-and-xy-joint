Why have each X/Y joint consist of two plastic parts and an end of the aluminium beam, all bolted together, when you can have it all as one plastic part? I think this improves rigidity, with some caveats. And then the fact that this allows the use of an additional X rail (without having to raise the gantry like in other mods) offers clear advantages for toolhead rigidity.

The latest commits use the standard Voron 0 bearings (much more convenient) rather than the larger ones shown in the Youtube video - I thought those might improve some horizontal ripples / VFAs I was getting, but they didn't. These latest changes have not yet been tested, but will be soon.

Other space constraints / collisions shown in the video are fixed too, and tested.

The design increased the stiffness of the toolhead / X carriage significantly, although interestingly a year or two later, it seems very wobbly again and this appears due to the ABS plastic of this part becoming less rigid. I'm going to try printing it again, and will use close to 100% infil from now on. Printing it in a carbon fibre filament would also be interesting.
